## English

Indrawan Lisanto - known online as **indrawandev** - is an AI-Native Full-Stack Engineer who works mobile-first: over 6+ years of shipping React Native apps and web/backend systems for banking, fintech, telecommunications, and enterprise teams.

He believes the future of software engineering is not about writing more code, but about designing better systems where humans and AI work together. His daily work follows that belief: he designs the architecture and the workflow, while AI agents execute most of the coding - agent orchestration, specification-driven development, and terminal-first engineering.

He maintains open-source projects such as **omotg** (a zero-dependency Go bridge between Telegram and coding agents) and **superspec-ai-workflow**, and is building a community for Indonesian developers exploring AI-native engineering.

Build faster. Spend less. Ship more.
